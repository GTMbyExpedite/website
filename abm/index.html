What this series covers

The full operating motion

AI has made the mechanical half of account-based marketing nearly free. Enrichment, signal capture, drafting at scale. Picking the right 100 accounts and knowing when to act on what those tools surface is still a judgement call. Most teams skip straight past that on their way to the tooling.

The usual fix once founder-led sales stops scaling is to buy an enterprise stack and hire a sales development team. This series skips that path. Five practitioners hand over the motion on camera: who to target, when to act, how to stay warm, and how to run all of it every week. Tools and judgement calls that work without a big budget.

Each session is a real conversation. Both sides put their own client work on the table, so you get the trade-offs alongside the theory. Every session answers the question the one before it raised.
