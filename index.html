The Saturation Method

How we build your pipeline, in four stages

Spread yourself across a whole market and you dilute. Concentrate on a focused set of accounts and they saturate into pipeline. Your team runs it after we've gone.

01

Focus

Nail the ICP and pick the accounts that actually matter: your first 100, worked hard, rather than fifty thousand reached badly.

02

Build

Stand up the data, the infrastructure and the messaging that make those accounts reachable in the first place.

03

Activate

Signal-led, AI-backed outreach built around your big rocks: events, launches, the moments buyers already care about.

04

Compound

Align sales and marketing on one list, document it, and hand the engine to your team so it keeps running.

Fewer channels, run harder. AI does the reach; people keep the taste, deciding what good looks like and what ships. Our outbound, event and email maturity models all nest inside these four stages, so you always know which level you're operating at.
